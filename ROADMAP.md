# Vision

Pradigi is building the world's first AI-native Learning Operating System.

Our roadmap is not about adding more features.

It is about gradually replacing every fragmented learning tool with one intelligent operating system that understands, teaches, and grows alongside every learner.

# Long-Term Vision

```
                 PRADIGI

                     │

           Learning Identity

                     │

              AI Intelligence

                     │

       ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐

    Academy      Workspace      Career

       │             │             │

   Projects      Portfolio     Employment

       └─────────────┼─────────────┘

          Lifelong Learning OS
```

Pradigi begins as an AI Academy.

It evolves into an AI Workspace.

It matures into an AI Learning Operating System.

Its ultimate role is to become the infrastructure that helps anyone, anywhere, continuously learn, prove their skills, and connect to real-world opportunities throughout their lifetime.

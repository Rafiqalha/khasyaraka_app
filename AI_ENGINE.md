# Vision

Pradigi AI is not a chatbot.

It is a Learning Intelligence Engine.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that answer questions, Pradigi continuously understands, models, teaches, evaluates, and improves every learner.

The AI never starts from zero.

Every interaction becomes part of the learner's evolving intelligence model.
